A Professional Executive's Golden Rules

Ten non-negotiable principles for every jewellery professional

  1. Never provide incorrect product information.
  2. Never confuse Karat with Carat.
  3. Never hide gemstone treatments.
  4. Always explain pricing transparently.
  5. Always follow the store's security procedures.
  6. Never promise an unauthorised discount.
  7. Always verify product details before billing.
  8. Treat every customer with respect.
  9. Never make unsupported claims about gemstones or their healing properties.
  10. Keep learning about new jewellery designs, gemstones and industry standards.

Professional Golden Rules for Sales Executives

জুয়েলারি সেলস এক্সিকিউটিভদের জন্য সোনালি নিয়ম

  1. Never force a customer to buy. – গ্রাহককে কখনো জোর করে পণ্য কেনাবেন না।
  2. Always understand the customer's budget. – গ্রাহকের বাজেট বুঝুন।
  3. Sell benefits, not just product features. – শুধু বৈশিষ্ট্য নয়, সুবিধাও বোঝান।
  4. Never make false promises. – মিথ্যা প্রতিশ্রুতি দেবেন না।
  5. Explain pricing transparently. – দাম নির্ধারণের বিষয়গুলি পরিষ্কারভাবে বুঝিয়ে বলুন।
  6. Never hide product limitations. – পণ্যের সীমাবদ্ধতা লুকাবেন না।
  7. Maintain customer confidentiality. – গ্রাহকের তথ্যের গোপনীয়তা বজায় রাখুন।
  8. Follow up professionally. – পেশাদারভাবে Follow-up করুন।
  9. Focus on customer satisfaction, not just sales targets. – শুধু টার্গেট নয়, গ্রাহকের সন্তুষ্টির ওপর গুরুত্ব দিন।
  10. Build relationships, not just transactions. – শুধু বিক্রয় নয়, দীর্ঘমেয়াদি সম্পর্ক তৈরি করুন।

Scientific Properties at a Glance

The values below are typical reference ranges for natural gemstones. Slight variations may occur based on chemical composition, origin, treatments, and testing methods.

GemstoneRI (Approx)SG (Approx)Mohs HardnessMineral / Composition

What do these terms mean?

  • RI – Refractive Index: A measure of how much light bends when entering the gemstone.
  • SG – Specific Gravity: The ratio of the gemstone's density compared to water of the same volume.
  • Mohs Hardness: The relative ability to resist scratching. It is not the same as toughness or resistance to breaking.
  • Diamond 4Cs: Cut, Colour, Clarity, and Carat Weight.

Important for Sales Staff

Never confirm a gemstone's identity based solely on colour or visual inspection. Accurate identification may require a trained gemologist, appropriate instruments, and reliable laboratory reports.

If a gemstone has treatments, synthetic origins, alternative stones, or assembled components, accurate information must be disclosed according to applicable laws, store policies, and reports.

Weight Conversion

Carat vs Ratti: Understand the Difference

Carat (ct) is the internationally recognized unit for gemstone weight. Ratti is a traditional Indian weight unit, and its practical value can vary by region and business practice.

5 Real Customer Scenarios

Scenario 01 · Budget-Conscious Customer"I want a real gemstone, but my budget is low." Guide them to suitable alternatives without compromising on accurate information.
Scenario 02 · Astrology-Believing Customer"Which stone will guarantee my problems are solved?" Respect their beliefs, but never guarantee astrological results or make scientifically unproven claims.
Scenario 03 · Certificate-Seeking Customer"How do I know if this stone is natural?" Explain reports, laboratories, treatments, and the limitations of reports in simple terms.
Scenario 04 · Comparing Two Stones"Why is this Ruby more expensive than the other?" Explain colour, clarity, treatments, origin claims, cut, and documentation.
Scenario 05 · Daily Wear Suitability"Can I wear this stone every day?" Discuss hardness, brittleness, setting protection, and care instructions.

Trainer's Daily Checklist

  • Can the trainee name the gemstone and its mineral family?
  • Can they explain RI, SG, and hardness in simple terms?
  • Can they explain the difference between hardness and brittleness?
  • Can they provide accurate information about treatments and certificates?
  • Can they explain care instructions and answer customer questions?

Professional Selling Principles

Build trust through accurate information, transparency, and attentive listening. Never guarantee astrological results, investment returns, rarity, or origin without reliable proof.
